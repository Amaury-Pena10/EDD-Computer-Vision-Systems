# Design Brief - Face Recognition

## Problem Statement
Facial recognition helps people and organizations quickly verify or identify induvisuals
for setcurity, access, and convience.

## Proposed Solution
Our system uses facial recognition to identify or verify people by analyzing their
facial features and matching them with authorized profiles, helping provide a faster, more convenient, and secure way to control access and verify identities.

## CV Technique(s) Used
The main computer vision technique used is face recognition because it can detect
and identify a person by analyzing their facial features and comparing them to 
known faces, making it a good fit for secure and convenient identify verification
.

## Success Criteria
The sytem should correctly recognize an authorized person's face within 2 meters
with at least 90% accuracy across 10 trials, while avoiding false identification of unauthorized people.
